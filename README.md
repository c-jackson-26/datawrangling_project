### Project Purpose:

This project will be used to better understand the impacts of education on chronic disease. According to the  Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation, "18 years of education reduces the risk of death by 34%." This project intends to explore the chronic illnesses most prominent in states with poorly ranked public education systems.
